Different crops.
Different needs.

Choose a crop to start exploring relevant crop-care information. For diagnosis and product use, consult a qualified local agriculture expert and follow the label.

SELECTED CROP ADVISORY

🌾 Rice (Paddy)

Key Stages: Basal / Transplanting (0–15 DAT), Active Tillering (20–40 DAT), Panicle Initiation (50–65 DAT).

Watch Out For: Early weed competition, zinc/nutrient lockup, stem borer, and leaf folder.

Suggested Solutions (Temp): RootMax GR (Basal), KisanWeed Clean (15–25 DAT), Humistar + AgriShield Duo.
